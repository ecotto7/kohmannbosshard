OUR HISTORY

Inside the Box

Kohmann Bosshard Financial Services was established in 2006 by Otto Bosshard to provide exceptional investment management to high-net-worth individuals and organizations. Otto aptly coined his approach “investing inside the box,” and created a strong foundation of structured and disciplined financial strategies. 

Tailored Financial Solutions

Dean Kohmann has led the Kohmann Bosshard team since 2015, advancing Otto’s vision. Under Dean’s leadership, Kohmann Bosshard has expanded to offer comprehensive wealth management, financial planning, and estate planning. Today, we proudly serve families, trusts, estates, retirement plans, and charitable organizations with tailored financial solutions.

DEAN KOHMANN | CPA Inactive

President

Dean is the President of Kohmann Bosshard. He has worked in the financial services industry since 1991, including as a CPA at Ernst & Young, LLP; 14 years as a VP of Charles Schwab, and 4 years as SVP of ValMark Financial Group before joining Otto Bosshard and founding Kohmann Bosshard. He earned his Accounting degree from Case Western Reserve University. He has been married to Diana since 1994 and has three wonderful daughters.

OTTO BOSSHARD | CFA®

Former Chairman

Otto was the Chairman of Kohmann Bosshard. He was the former Vice President, Treasurer and Chief Investment Officer of Westfield Insurance Group, a former member of the Board of Directors at Westfield Bank and ValMark Financial Group, and former member of the Investment Committee of Summa Health System. He graduated from the University of Cincinnati before beginning his over 40 years in this industry. He is survived by his wife Claudia Bosshard of 54 years, as well as his two sons and five grandchildren.
